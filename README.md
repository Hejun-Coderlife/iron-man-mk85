# Iron Man · Mark 85 (web)

A browser game built with three.js: walk, fly and fight as Iron Man Mark 85 in a desert village or in Midtown Manhattan.

**Play:** https://hejun-coderlife.github.io/iron-man-mk85/

Controls: WASD move/fly · Space take off · C land · Shift boost · ⌥/Alt or double-tap WASD dodge · Mouse aim · LMB repulsor · RMB or R unibeam · Q forearm laser (hold) · E shoulder guns · F snap · B summon boss (desert) · M mute

Desert: destroy the weapon depots (one is hidden in a cave) to bring out the boss. Outposts are guarded by soldiers — kill the officer and the rest break and surrender; stop radio operators before they call in support, and watch out for EMP grenades and RPGs. Day turns to dusk and night as you play.
New York: Ultron arrives on the landmark tower's helipad at wave 4.
